# Changelog

## 1.1

The four themes are repainted to match their references more closely: two pieces of key art from a certain game set somewhere in sunny Florida, whose name shall not be spoken.

* Bay Light: the bay in daylight. A sky blue frame, mint water panels, a sand editor, lavender palm trees on the status bar and peach facades in the selection.
* Bay: the same bay at nightfall, with pastel tokens: aqua, lavender, sky blue, peach, sand and pink.
* Sunset Light: the sky at dusk, from a lavender frame to pink panels and a peach editor, with plum palm trees on the status bar.
* Sunset: plum backgrounds instead of navy, a peach status bar, peach functions and pale yellow strings.
* The neon glow follows the new token colors.

## 1.0

* Four themes for Visual Studio 2019, 2022 and 2026: Miami '26 Bay, Bay Light, Sunset and Sunset Light.
* A neon glow around keywords, types, functions, tags and variables in the dark themes, with its brightness set in *Tools > Options > Miami '26 > Neon Glow*.
